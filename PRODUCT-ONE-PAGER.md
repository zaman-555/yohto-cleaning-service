# Extra Team Dashboard
### Workforce scheduling for cleaning & field teams — one page overview

**Live demo:** https://app.pcsmonthlypla.online

---

## The problem

Spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and paper rosters break down when your team grows. Staff miss shifts, admins re-enter the same job details every week, and nobody has one trusted place to see **who works where, when, and how**.

## The solution

**Extra Team Dashboard** is a secure web app that gives your team:

- A **monthly schedule grid** — every person × every day, with shift times, company, task, car, status, and location.
- A **weekly job showcase** — detailed site instructions (keys, alarm, equipment, max hours) with columns you can customize.
- **Automatic hour summaries** — monthly totals, weekly averages, and daily team hours.

No app store install. Works on phone, tablet, and desktop.
