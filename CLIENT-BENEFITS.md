## The short pitch (30 seconds)

> Today your team plans work in a spreadsheet or chat. That works until someone has the wrong version, a vacation is missed, or staff can’t find their job on the phone.  
> **Extra Team Dashboard** gives everyone one secure place: the monthly plan, weekly site details, personal job cards, and automatic hour totals — on phone or computer, with login and your data on your server.

---

## Pain today → benefit with Extra Team

| What clients struggle with | How Extra Team helps |
|----------------------------|----------------------|
| Spreadsheet is hard to read on a phone | **My work** shows only that person’s jobs as clear cards |
| Many people edit the same sheet — mistakes and overwrites | Each change saves to the **database**; one source of truth |
| Staff ask “where do I go?” in WhatsApp | Shift, company, task, car, status, and **map link** in one place |
| Site details (keys, alarm, equipment) live in another tab or message | **Weekly showcase** holds the week’s instructions with columns you can customize |
| Counting hours for payroll takes time | Hours are calculated from shift times — daily totals, monthly per person, **Staff KPI** chart |
| New people need Google access or a shared password | Staff **register**; admin **approves**; each person has their own login |
| Forgotten passwords block work | **Forgot password** emails a reset link to their registered email |
| Growing team = more columns and confusion | Same process at 5 or 50 people — no per-seat fee inside the app |

## Benefits by role (say this to the decision maker)

### Field staff
- Open the app on their phone — no App Store install.
- See **today’s** and this month’s jobs under **My work**.
- Know shift time, site/task, vehicle, status, and location without asking the office.
- Border colour matches status type so jobs are easy to scan.

### Schedulers / admins
- Plan the whole month on one **Main dashboard** (day × person) — same idea as their monthly sheet, but structured.
- Fill **Weekly showcase** once for keys, alarm, instructions, equipment.
- Review everyone’s cards under **Staffs**.
- Check hours vs a working limit on **Staff KPI** (e.g. 170 h / person).
- Approve new users from the sidebar.

### Office / payroll / operations
- Automatic **hour summaries** instead of re-adding cell times.
- Clear history of past months (navigate months — data stays saved).
- Fewer “I didn’t know I was booked” conversations.

### Business owner
- One trusted schedule for the company.
- Data on **your server** (private), accessed over HTTPS.
- Scales with the team without paying per user inside the software.
- Professional image vs a shared spreadsheet link.

## Compared with “NEW Monthly plan” style spreadsheets

Clients often already use a coloured Google Sheet (green = normal job, orange = special site, red VACATION, week tabs, TOT. HOURS).

| Spreadsheet | Extra Team app |
|-------------|----------------|
| Fast free-text for planners who live in Sheets | Structured fields → reliable phone view and hour maths |
| Colours mean job/site/status | Colours mean **status type** (PCS Driving, Vacation, Sick leave, etc.) |
| Hard for field staff on mobile | Built for phone (**My work**) |
| Sharing = Google permissions | Real accounts + password reset |
| Formulas can break | Hours come from shift start–end automatically |

**Message for the client:**  
*The app keeps the same monthly planning idea you already know — who works which day — and adds mobile clarity, security, weekly site detail, and automatic hours that spreadsheets don’t give you cleanly.*
